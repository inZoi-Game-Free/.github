## 🛠️ What is inZOI?  
**inZOI** is a life simulation game where you become the *creator* of a vibrant world. Shape environments, influence personalities, and witness stories that unfold through the free will of its inhabitants—**Zois**.  

🌍 **Key Features**:  
- 🧬 **Personality-Driven Simulation**: Zois make choices based on traits, memories, and relationships.  
- 🏡 **Build & Customize**: Design homes, neighborhoods, and characters with easy-to-use tools.  
- 🌟 **Dynamic Events**: Unexpected twists like rumors, trends, and diseases emerge naturally.  
- 💞 **Emotional Depth**: Experience joy, heartbreak, ambition, and growth through Zois' lives.  

*"We aim to inspire players to appreciate life as the profound gift it is—a journey filled with meaning at every turn."*
